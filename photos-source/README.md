# Photo originals

Photographs sent by Simon & Joyce that are **not** used on the site.

This folder sits outside `public/`, so nothing here is served or deployed — it
is an archive, kept so a photo can be swapped in later without asking Nairobi
for it again. Anything that is actually on the site lives in `public/photos/`.

## `kitchen-originals/`

The remainder of the February–August 2026 WhatsApp batch.

- `PHOTO-2026-08-08-10-47-20*.jpg` — six more frames of the old way of cooking:
  an open fire on bare ground, and the cook working in an iron-sheet lean-to
  with basins and jerrycans. One frame from this run
  (`PHOTO-2026-08-08-10-47-19.jpg`) is the site's `before.jpg`; these are the
  alternates.
- `PHOTO-2026-06-18-18-38-51 3.jpg`, `… 4.jpg` — the queue at the kitchen door
  again, both with a thumb across the lens.
- `WhatsApp Image 2026-08-08 at 10.47.*.jpeg` — byte-for-byte duplicates of two
  `PHOTO-…` files above, the same pictures saved twice by WhatsApp. Safe to
  delete.

## `property-originals/` — not committed

The July 2025 walk-round of the church and school: the gate and its signage,
the sanctuary and yard, the empty plot, the classrooms inside and out, and the
academy's van. Twenty-eight frames at 4096×3072, about 191 MB.

**This one directory is in `.gitignore`.** Everything else under `photos-source/`
is committed; 191 MB of phone JPEGs is not, and it does not need to be — the
eleven the site uses are resized to 1600px and committed under `public/photos/`.
It arrived as `public/church and school property/`, which would have deployed
every megabyte of it. If you clear this folder, the originals are gone from the
machine, so keep the phone copy.

The seventeen not used are mostly near-duplicates of the classroom interiors,
plus the ablution block and a service alley — kept in case a page ever wants
them.

## `presentation-slides/`

Cropped out of `public/presentations/Jepegomi-Presentation.pptx`. The deck is
twenty flattened 1920×1080 slide images with the text baked in, so every
photograph on the site that came from it is a crop of the region the text does
not cover. The rest of those crops are in `public/photos/`.

- `church-sanctuary-full.jpg` — the whole sanctuary, front and side. A better
  view of the building than the wide banner the Church page uses, but the wrong
  shape for that slot. Swap it in if the page ever wants a squarer photo.
- `academy-whole-school.jpg` — the entire school stood on the grass. Not used
  only because three photographs was the right number for that strip.
- `slide19-school-van-listing.jpg` — **do not publish.** This is a classifieds
  listing photo of a van the ministry does not own, watermarked "MARTIN MAWIRA
  / POSTED ON JIJI". Slide 19 says the van fund reached $5,000 of the $12,000
  needed and no van has been bought, so the picture was an illustration in the
  deck. On the site it would be both someone else's photograph and a claim that
  is not true. Kept here only so nobody extracts it again wondering why it was
  skipped.
